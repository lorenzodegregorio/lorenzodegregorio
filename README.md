# Lorenzo De Gregorio

Mathematical Engineering graduate and MSc Data Science and Engineering student at Politecnico di Torino.

I am interested in applied data science for biomedical data, human performance, metabolomics, sport science, simulation and machine learning.

I am currently completing my Master’s thesis in collaboration with TU Dortmund and Biolyz, working on capillary blood metabolomics, VO₂-related indicators and the metabolic response to acute exercise.
